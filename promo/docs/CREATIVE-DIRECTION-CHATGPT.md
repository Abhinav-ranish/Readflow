# Readflow Promo — ChatGPT Version: Creative Direction

## Overall Concept: "The AI That Shares"

A 22-second product teaser showing one seamless flow: a user gives ChatGPT Codex a single prompt → GPT inspects a research project, writes a polished README, discovers the Readflow API, and shares it — all automatically. The viewer watches GPT *think, explore, and publish* without any extra steps. The payoff is "WiSpec" — a polished Wi-Fi sensing research page live on Readflow.

**Tone**: Sharp, fluid, modern. Clean intelligence. No hype.
**Palette**: Near-black (#0a0a0a–#171717), cool white text (#e8e8e8), OpenAI green accent (#10a37f).
**Typography**: SF Pro Display / Inter. Light (300) for ambient text, Semibold (600) for emphasis. Tight negative tracking.

## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: HOOK (0:00–0:03, frames 0–89)
- **Visual**: Dark screen. Two lines of text fade in from center with subtle upward drift.
- **On-screen text**: "Your AI just learned / **something new.**"
- **Motion**: Text opacity 0→1 over 0.6s, 20px upward ease. Thin green accent line springs in below. Subtle green radial glow pulses behind text. Full scene fades out in final 0.6s.
- **VO option**: *"Your AI just learned something new."*

### Scene 2: THE PROMPT (0:03–0:05.5, frames 90–164)
- **Visual**: ChatGPT Codex idle screen with "Let's build wifi_sensing_research" and the user's prompt at bottom. Slides up from bottom with spring physics. Green-tinted pill badge above: "ONE PROMPT. THAT'S IT."
- **On-screen text**: Badge only — the screenshot carries the prompt naturally.
- **Motion**: Spring slide-up (damping 50, stiffness 80). Scale 0.92→0.85. Green box-shadow glow. Exit fade.
- **VO option**: *"One prompt. That's all it takes."*
- **Key detail**: The prompt reads "Write a Readme to showcase what this research does... share it via readflow.aranish.uk"

### Scene 3: AI WORKING (0:05.5–0:11, frames 165–329)
- **Visual**: Montage of 3 screenshots cycling (thinking → exploring files → curl commands / sharing). Each slides in from right, holds ~1.8s, slides out left.
- **On-screen text**: Status labels at bottom with pulsing green dot: "THINKING..." → "EXPLORING..." → "SHARING..."
- **Motion**: Spring slide transitions (damping 40). Subtle sine-wave float when active. 3-segment progress bar filling left to right.
- **VO option**: *"It thinks. It explores. It shares."*
- **Screenshots used**: 03-chatgpt → 04-chatgpt → 05-chatgpt

### Scene 4: DONE (0:11–0:14, frames 330–419)
- **Visual**: Screenshot 06 — GPT's completion with "Worked for 2m 26s" and the Readflow link. Slow zoom from 0.82→1.1, panning up to center on the link. Green highlight glow around the URL.
- **On-screen text**: "Shared. **Automatically.**" at bottom.
- **Motion**: Spring entrance. Ken Burns zoom into the link. Highlight box fades in. Text springs from below.
- **VO option**: *"Shared. Automatically."*
- **Key detail**: Shows readflow.aranish.uk/s/8NJ-qfw3kU and "verified with HTTP 200"

### Scene 5: THE RESULT (0:14–0:17.5, frames 420–524)
- **Visual**: Screenshot 07 — the polished Readflow page for "WiSpec" research. Wrapped in browser chrome (dots + URL bar). Slow Ken Burns zoom 0.88→0.95 with parallax drift.
- **On-screen text**: "From thought to published flow." (top, muted)
- **Motion**: Fade-in 0.5s. Gentle drift. Exit fade. Browser bar shows `readflow.aranish.uk/s/8NJ-qfw3kU`.
- **VO option**: *"From thought... to published flow."*

### Scene 6: CTA (0:17.5–0:22, frames 525–659)
- **Visual**: Clean dark screen. Readflow wordmark (green "R" square + "Readflow" text). Two-line tagline. Green accent line. URL.
- **On-screen text**:
  - "Your AI writes."
  - "**Readflow shares.**"
  - readflow.aranish.uk
- **Motion**: Tagline springs in first. Accent line expands. URL fades. Logo enters last with scale spring. Green background glow builds. Holds.
- **VO option**: *"Your AI writes. Readflow shares."*

## 3 Alternate Hook Options (first 3 seconds)

### Hook A (current): "Your AI just learned something new."
Intrigue-based. Universal. Works for any AI. Calm, slightly mysterious.

### Hook B: "What if ChatGPT could share its work?"
Direct question. Names the AI. Positions GPT as a collaborator that now has a new capability.

### Hook C: "Watch. One prompt. Done."
Challenge hook. Bold, compressed. Speed is the selling point.

---

## 3 Alternate CTA/End-Frame Options

### CTA A (current): "Your AI writes. Readflow shares."
Clean split. Two actions, two actors. Universal.

### CTA B: "Let GPT send the flow."
Single line. Action-oriented. Names ChatGPT directly. Developer-friendly.

### CTA C: "From GPT to anyone. Instantly."
Emphasizes the sharing/distribution angle. Research goes from GPT's output to a live link.
